AI‑First BFSI A North Star Operating Model
An operating model, not a technology roadmap

What the best BFSI company looks like when agents do the work.

A working picture of a large Indian NBFC run AI‑first: every stage of the customer lifecycle and every enterprise function staffed by named AI agents, with human judgment placed deliberately — where the regulator requires it, where the customer deserves it, and nowhere else.

Read this as a design target. An independent, illustrative concept model of a generic diversified Indian lender — not affiliated with, endorsed by, or describing any specific company, and not a commitment by anyone. Metrics shown are directional benchmarks for debate, not measured results. Every regulatory reference is indicative and must be validated against current RBI, SEBI, IRDAI and MeitY texts with qualified compliance and legal counsel before any build.

How to use this page. Walk the lifecycle rail left to right and ask one question at each stage: would we be comfortable defending this autonomy level to a regulator, a customer, and our own audit committee? Where the answer is no, the fix is usually a control, not a retreat from automation.
